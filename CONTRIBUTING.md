# Contributing

Issues and technical feedback are welcome for interoperability review.

Pull requests may be declined unless separate contribution terms are approved. Please do not submit confidential information, proprietary implementation details, non-public legal mapping materials, commercial strategy, non-public commercial materials, production credentials, payment data, security-sensitive secrets, or third-party material you are not authorized to contribute.

Discussion on IETF mailing lists, at an IETF Hackathon, or in other IETF venues is governed by the applicable IETF processes and policies. Repository activity is governed by this repository's evaluation-only terms unless separately agreed in writing.
